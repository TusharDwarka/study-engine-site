study_engine privacy policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

study_engine is a personal tool that its owner runs on their own computer.

What it reads

  • From Google Classroom: courses, coursework, course materials and announcements.
  • From Google Drive: the files attached to those Classroom items. The permission granted is read-only access to Drive.

It cannot change or delete anything in Classroom or Drive.

Where the data goes

  • Downloaded items and the Google sign-in token are stored on the owner's computer. study_engine has no server of its own.
  • Course material is sent to Anthropic's Claude AI service to make summaries, explanations and study plans.
  • Task titles, descriptions and due dates may be saved to the owner's own task app, which stores them in Google Firebase.

The data is not sold and is not used for advertising.

Deleting data and removing access

Delete the tool's folder to remove the downloaded files and the sign-in token, and delete any tasks in the task app. Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
